# Privacy Policy for OmniPDF

**Last Updated: September 4, 2026**

OmniPDF ("OmniPDF", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the OmniPDF mobile application (the "App").

## 1. Your Documents and PDF Files

OmniPDF is designed to process your documents directly on your device.

When you use OmniPDF to create, convert, edit, merge, split, compress, or otherwise process a document, the files are processed locally on your device unless a feature specifically states otherwise.

**We do not upload your documents or PDF files to our servers as part of the normal operation of the App.**

We do not access, read, sell, or use the contents of your documents for advertising or other unrelated purposes.

Because your documents are processed locally, the security of those files is also affected by the security of your device, its operating system, and any other applications that have access to your files.

## 2. Information We May Collect

OmniPDF itself is designed to minimize the collection of personal information.

Depending on the features and services you use, certain information may nevertheless be processed by third-party service providers, including advertising and payment providers.

This may include information such as:

- Device and operating-system information.
- App version and basic technical information.
- Advertising identifiers or similar device identifiers.
- Information about advertisements that are displayed or interacted with.
- Purchase and subscription information necessary to process in-app purchases.
- Diagnostic, crash, or performance information where such services are enabled.

The specific information collected may depend on the third-party services used by the App and the settings of your device.

## 3. Advertising

OmniPDF may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks.

Advertising providers may collect or receive certain information from your device in order to provide, measure, personalize, or limit advertisements. This information may include device identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, advertising information, and interactions with advertisements, depending on the advertising provider and your device settings.

**Your documents and the contents of your PDF files are not provided to advertising networks by OmniPDF.**

Where required by applicable law or platform requirements, we will request appropriate consent before using information for personalized advertising.

You may also be able to limit or disable personalized advertising through your device's privacy settings.

## 4. In-App Purchases

OmniPDF may offer paid features, subscriptions, or other in-app purchases.

Purchases are processed through the relevant app store or payment platform, such as Google Play or Apple's App Store.

We do not receive or store your full payment-card details through OmniPDF.

The applicable app store may provide us with limited purchase information necessary to verify purchases, provide purchased features, manage subscriptions, prevent fraud, or provide customer support.

Your purchases are also subject to the terms and privacy policies of the applicable app store.

## 5. Information Stored on Your Device

OmniPDF may store certain information locally on your device to provide its features.

This may include:

- Application settings and preferences.
- Information required to remember purchased or unlocked features.
- Recently used files or other application-related information, where applicable.
- Temporary files required to perform PDF operations.

The exact information stored depends on the features you use and your device's operating system.

You can generally remove locally stored application information by uninstalling the App or using the relevant controls provided by your device.

## 6. Third-Party Services

OmniPDF may use third-party services to provide advertising, in-app purchases, diagnostics, analytics, or other functionality.

These third-party providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Examples may include services provided by:

- Google Play
- Apple App Store
- Third-party advertising providers
- Crash reporting or analytics providers, if enabled

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of the relevant third-party providers to understand how they process information.

## 7. Children's Privacy

OmniPDF is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to a service associated with OmniPDF inappropriately, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

## 8. Data Security

Because OmniPDF processes documents locally on your device, your documents generally remain under your control and are not transmitted to our servers during normal PDF processing.

We nevertheless recommend keeping your device and operating system up to date and using appropriate device security measures such as a screen lock or device encryption where available.

No software or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your device, operating system, location, and applicable law, you may have choices concerning:

- Personalized advertising.
- Advertising identifiers.
- App permissions.
- Access to files and documents.
- Purchase and subscription information.
- Certain analytics or diagnostic data.

You can manage many of these choices through your device or app-store privacy settings.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when OmniPDF's features, services, or data practices change.

If we make material changes, we may provide appropriate notice through the App or another reasonable method.

The "Last Updated" date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy indicates when this policy was last revised.
